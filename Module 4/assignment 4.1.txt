2.Create a simple HTML file named myprofile.html that displays your name, a short bio, and a profile picture using appropriate HTML tags (h1, p, img). Open it in your browser and view the result.

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">
    <title>Shweta's Profile</title>
</head>
<body>
    <h1>Shweta</h1>

    <p>Hello! My name is Shweta. I am learning HTML and enjoy creating simple webpages.</p>

    <img src="profile-picture.jpeg" alt="Profile picture of Shweta" width="200" height="200">
</body>
</html>
